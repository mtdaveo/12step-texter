12th Step Texter — Area 40, District 11

Current vs. Proposed Method

How A.A. contact requests flow from facilities to volunteers — before and after 12th Step Texter.

The current District 11 12th Step protocol works — but it depends on the A.A. Coordinator as a relay point for every request. 12th Step Texter removes that bottleneck, connecting facilities directly to volunteers while keeping the coordinator in full control of who is on the list.

Step Current Method 12th Step Texter
Volunteer List Creation A.A. Coordinator collects paper forms from members willing to volunteer. Manually compiles and maintains phone number lists for men and women. A.A. Coordinator enters approved volunteer numbers directly into the app. Same human vetting process — digitized entry only.
List Maintenance Coordinator updates paper lists and must notify all facilities of any changes to the contact number or protocol. Coordinator updates the app and hits Save. Change is live instantly for every future request. No calls to facilities, no reprinting.
Facility → Coordinator Facility staff calls or texts the A.A. Coordinator's dedicated phone number with patient information — in whatever format they choose.
⏱ Minutes to hours depending on availability 		Facility staff scans a QR code, fills out a structured form, and hits Send. Request goes directly to all volunteers simultaneously.
⚡ Seconds
Coordinator → Volunteers Coordinator receives the request, then forwards or relays it to the volunteer group text — manually, when available.
⏱ Minutes to hours — adds to total delay 		This step is eliminated. The facility form sends directly to all volunteers.
✓ Eliminated
Message Format Unstructured — varies by facility staff member. Key information (room number, age, gender) often missing or inconsistently formatted. Consistent, structured message every time. Required fields enforced. Volunteers always receive the same clean format.
Patient Consent Gate Depends on facility staff to communicate whether the patient personally requested the visit. Not standardized. Required confirmation checkbox — the form cannot be submitted without it. Built in, cannot be bypassed.
The bottom line: The coordinator's role in vetting and managing volunteers remains unchanged. What changes is the request pathway — facility staff no longer need to track down a phone number, and the coordinator is no longer a required relay point for every individual request. Requests that currently take minutes to hours reach volunteers in seconds.
