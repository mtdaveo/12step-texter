12th Step Texter

Information for A.A. Volunteers

What This Is

If you've agreed to be on the District 11 12th Step call list, this is how requests will come to you going forward.

What to Expect

When a patient at a local treatment facility requests a visit from AA, you'll receive a group text from a facility staff member. It will look something like this:

12TH STEP REQUEST...
Facility: Riverstone Health
Room: 214
Name: JAMES R
Info: 34 yr old Male

Clean, consistent, all the information you need.

How to Respond

Reply to the thread as you normally would. Everyone on the list can see the replies — so if you're able to go, say so. If someone else has already responded, you'll see that too. No duplicate visits, no dropped balls.

There's nothing new you need to do or install. The texts come to your regular phone number just as they always have.

A Note on the Request

Every request that comes through this system includes a confirmation from the facility staff that the patient personally asked for A.A. contact. That step is built into the form and cannot be skipped. You can respond with confidence that you're walking into a situation where someone has asked for help.

Questions

If you have questions about the list — joining, leaving, updating your number — reach out to the A.A. contact as listed at the top of the Request form. The app is managed on the A.A. side, not by the facilities.

Thank you for your service.
