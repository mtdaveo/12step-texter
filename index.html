A.A. Contact Request System

⚠ Testing Site

This is a demonstration and testing environment, not the final deployment. The app is fully functional here for evaluation purposes.

To test the full flow: complete the A.A. Coordinator Setup, select a district and city, hit Save Configuration, then Generate QR Code. Scan the QR with your phone — the Facility form will load with the current volunteer list pulled live from the cloud.

Volunteer list changes take effect immediately on save — no need to redistribute the QR code.
