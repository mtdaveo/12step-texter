# 12th Step Texter — Instructions for Facility Staff

## What This Is

12th Step Texter is a simple tool that connects your patients with Alcoholics Anonymous volunteers. When a patient requests a visit from AA, this form sends their information directly to local AA volunteers by text — in seconds, from your phone.

---

## How to Submit a Request

**Step 1 — Open the form**
Scan the QR code posted at your station, or tap the link provided by your AA contact. The form opens directly in your phone's browser — nothing to download or install.  NOTE: The list of volunteers may be updated, so it is best to use the QR code (instead of searching past text threads) every time.

**Step 2 — Select Male or Female**
This routes the request to the correct AA volunteer list.

**Step 3 — Fill in the patient information**
- Facility name
- Room number (# only, optional - or use Notes below)
- Patient first name and last initial
- Patient age
- Notes (optional)

**Step 4 — Confirm the patient's request**
Check the box confirming that **the patient personally asked for AA contact.** In our experience, one who makes the request themselves is in a better place to benefit from our visit.

**Step 5 — Hit Send**
Your phone's text messaging app will open with all the volunteer numbers and the patient information already filled in. Tap Send. That's it.

---

## A Few Things Worth Knowing

**Always use the QR code or link to submit a new request.**
Again, your phone may save the group text thread from previous submissions. Please don't use that thread to send new requests — volunteer lists change, and an old thread may not reach everyone. The QR code always opens a fresh, current form.

**About the group text thread on your phone**
After your first submission, your phone will create a group text thread with the volunteer names or numbers. You may want to rename it something recognizable — on iPhone, tap the group name at the top of the thread and select "Change Name." On Android, tap the three dots or group name and look for "Group name." Something like "AA Billings Volunteers" works well. This is optional but can help avoid confusion with other threads.

**Responses come back to you.**
AA volunteers reply directly to the text thread. You'll see their responses and can pass along any relevant information to the patient.

---

## Questions

For questions about the form or the volunteer list, reach out to the A.A. contact as listed at the top of the Request form.



Thank you for supporting your patients in this way.
