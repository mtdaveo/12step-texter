12th Step Texter

Introduction for A.A. Coordinator (CPC Committee?)

The 12th Step Texter is a suggestion for a more automated, streamlined system of communications between A.A. and its volunteers and the professional community, geared toward facilities potentially serving alcoholics.

How It Works Now — District 11

Currently in District 11, when a patient at a facility requests A.A. contact, facility staff call or text the A.A. Coordinator's dedicated phone number with the patient's information. The coordinator then relays that request to the volunteer group text. This works — but it depends on the coordinator being available, and each relay step adds time. Requests that should take seconds can take minutes or hours.

12th Step Texter eliminates the relay. The facility submits directly to all volunteers simultaneously, in seconds, with a consistent structured message every time. See the full Current vs. Proposed comparison →

The Problem It Solves

Right now, when a patient at Riverstone or another facility asks to speak with someone from AA, a staff member has to pull up a group text thread on their personal phone, type out the patient's information, and hope their volunteer list is still current. It works — but it puts a lot of burden on facility staff who aren't part of AA, and there's no standard format, so messages often come through missing key information.

What This App Does

It's a simple web form that lives on a phone. Here's the flow:

  1. A QR code is posted at the nurses' station (or sent to the facility contact)
  2. Staff scans it — a clean, simple form opens on their phone
  3. They fill in: facility name, room number, patient name, age, any notes
  4. They select Male or Female (this routes the request to the right volunteer list)
  5. They check a box confirming the patient personally requested A.A. contact — this is required before the form will submit, and it can't be bypassed
  6. They hit Send — their phone opens a pre-written group text to all current A.A. volunteers with all the patient info filled in automatically
  7. They hit Send one more time — done

Upgrades from the current system:

1
Facility no longer needs to input and maintain A.A. volunteer phone numbers. We control that list and those numbers. When someone joins or leaves the list, the coordinator updates it once — the change is live immediately for every future request. No calls to facilities, no outdated threads, no need to redistribute a new QR code.
2
Facility no longer needs to create a text message with all the information we need. They simply fill out 3–5 fields. A text with all the information we need is generated and distributed to all A.A. volunteers on the list.

The volunteers receive a clean, consistent message. They can see each other's replies in the thread and coordinate from there, exactly as they do now.

What A.A. Coordinators Do

One person in the district sets up the volunteer lists (separate Men's and Women's lists) through a coordinator page. When volunteers join or leave, the coordinator updates the list and saves — the change is live instantly. The QR code at the facility never needs to be reprinted or redistributed.

See It Live

You can see the full working app here:

https://mtdaveo.github.io/12step-texter/

To test both sides, you'll need to play A.A. Coordinator and Facility from the same device.

A.A. Side
  1. Select District 11 / Billings from the Location dropdowns.
  2. Set up A.A. volunteers (phone numbers, names if you want). One list for males, another for females. This test is more visible if you include an additional phone number, but will work with your own.
  3. Optionally adjust the text message template using the token fields.
  4. Hit Save Configuration at bottom right. The volunteer list saves to the cloud instantly.
  5. Hit Generate QR Code. This QR points to a permanent Billings URL — it never needs to change.
Facility Side
  1. On your mobile phone, scan the QR code generated above. This opens the request form pre-loaded with the current volunteer list.
  2. Fill out the form for a sample patient.
  3. Hit Send Request to A.A. Volunteers.

You should receive a text to the phone numbers you provided on the A.A. Side regarding the patient you created on the Facility Side.

Where It Goes From Here

This is built and working. The next step is getting it in front of one facility — even just as a test — to see how staff respond to it. If it works in Billings, it could roll out across Area 40 for any district that wants it.

I think this is worth bringing to the committee. Happy to demo it in person whenever you'd like.
